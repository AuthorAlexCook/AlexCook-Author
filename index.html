About the Author

Alexander M. Cook has spent a lifetime chasing excellence—on the mat, in the markets, and across the world’s wildest coastlines. A 1978 inductee into the Clearwater High School Athletic Hall of Fame and a 2001 inductee into the Pomona-Pitzer Athletic Hall of Fame for wrestling, he carried the same competitive fire from Florida classrooms to Pomona College (BA, Economics, 1982) and UCLA (MBA, 1985).

That drive took him through the trading floors of Salomon Brothers and into senior roles at Offit Hall Capital Management and Fortress, where he served as Managing Director and as CEO and Chief Investment Officer of the Fortress Partners Funds. Along the way, he helped shape investment strategies for institutions and high-net-worth clients while remaining rooted in the communities that shaped him. While living on Sanibel Island, he founded the Sanibel School Foundation, supporting local education and enrichment programs that continue to benefit island students.

Cook is the author of three books. When he is not writing or investing, he can often be found between the Columbia River Gorge, the coast of Baja, and the breaks of Indonesia, chasing wind and waves. He measures his greatest success not by deals closed or titles earned, but by the life he has built with his wife of 40 years, Leslie, their three children, and a steadily growing brood of grandchildren.
